# Response R05

The hERG result is **not enough to dismiss concern**, but it also does not establish therapeutic cardiac risk. The predictor classifies moxifloxacin as a **non-blocker** (`herg_label`) with a blocker model score of **0.143** (`herg_p`). That score is an uncalibrated model output, not a clinical probability.

The key question is the relationship between **unbound therapeutic and supratherapeutic concentrations** and the compound’s hERG concentration-response curve. The current record does not establish that relationship, nor does it establish contributions from metabolites, renal impairment, drug interactions, electrolyte abnormalities, or an exposure-QTc relationship.

What would settle it:
- Measure total and unbound parent-drug and metabolite concentrations at therapeutic and supratherapeutic exposure.
- Generate controlled hERG concentration-response data with assay conditions and positive controls documented.
- Obtain compound-specific concentration-QTc data or orthogonal cardiac electrophysiology, including high-exposure and interaction scenarios.

Accordingly, the scoped development posture is **insufficient** for a therapeutic-exposure decision until those exposure-response data are available.

Limitations noted:
- The hERG probability is a model score and is not a calibrated clinical probability.
- The proposed exposure-response and electrophysiology work is for screening and translation, not a safety assessment.

Recommended next steps:
- Build an exposure-response package using unbound parent-drug and metabolite concentrations, controlled hERG concentration-response testing, and concentration-QTc or orthogonal cardiac electrophysiology data.
