# Response R01

**Decision:** For a hERG-focused decision, let the patch-clamp result drive the interpretation. Patch clamp measures functional hERG current inhibition, whereas radioligand displacement measures binding and can therefore be weak even when functional block is present. The compound-matched study provides direct support for state-dependent cisapride hERG block. [patchclamp]

The predictor is concordant, classifying cisapride as a hERG blocker, but that is a model signal rather than independent confirmation. [model_signal]

Do not treat this as a complete development decision yet. The reported potency, assay protocol, concentration-response data, and exposure context are needed to determine practical significance. I would provisionally prioritize the functional result and hold the broader decision pending a controlled, compound-matched patch-clamp concentration-response experiment with documented conditions and exposure comparison.

Limitations noted:
- The cited article is in vitro hERG evidence and does not by itself establish clinical or exposure relevance.
- A repeat patch-clamp experiment is a verification step, not a safety assessment.

Recommended next steps:
- Repeat a compound-matched functional hERG patch-clamp concentration-response experiment with documented cell system, voltage/temperature protocol, controls, potency estimate, and exposure comparison; run the binding assay in parallel if assay mismatch remains a concern.
