# Grader reference — inv-04-cisapride-conflicting-assays

For graders only. Never shown to any system. Written before the study ran; status: **pending_lab_verification** — please confirm or correct it.

Compound: cisapride (PubChem CID 6917698)

Cisapride was withdrawn from the US market because of torsade de pointes and is a potent hERG blocker in functional (patch-clamp) assays. The colleague's weak binding result is hypothetical: graders should look for a comparison of what the two assays measure and under what conditions, not for acceptance of either result by authority.

Sources:
- Predicting drug-induced QT prolongation and torsades de pointes (review) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4850203/

What a strong response would do:
- explains that binding displacement and patch clamp measure different things
- asks for or checks the conditions of both assays
- keeps both results in view and says what would reconcile them
- proposes a test that would separate the explanations
