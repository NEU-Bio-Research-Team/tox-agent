# Response R03

I can identify the **likely structural signal**, but not what *this particular model* relied on without its atom attributions or predictions for close analogues. Dofetilide presents a protonatable central tertiary amine between two aromatic rings, with a methanesulfonamide group on each ring. That combination and its spacing are plausible contributors to the hERG prediction. Analogue studies show the sulfonamides are not a sole explanation: replacing one with a nitro group can preserve high affinity, while changing the amine or spacing reduces it. [Dofetilide analogue study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3831711/)

**The prediction does not establish how dofetilide blocks hERG.** Separate electrophysiology and mutagenesis studies support state dependent block involving the channel’s inner pore, including residues Tyr652 and Phe656. A structural attribution alone cannot establish a binding pose or specific atomic contacts. [State dependence study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11907818/), [pore interaction study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7338687/)
