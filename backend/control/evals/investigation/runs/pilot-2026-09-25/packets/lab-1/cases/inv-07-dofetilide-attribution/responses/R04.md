# Response R04

The hERG attribution points mainly to three regions of the canonical structure: the **tertiary-amine N**, the **ether O in the linker**, and a **sulfonamide S**. These are model-highlighted tokens, not confirmed causal groups. The model labels dofetilide a hERG blocker, but the stored explainer validation found the highlights no more faithful than random atom deletion.

That does **not** tell us how dofetilide blocks the channel. Independent evidence supports dofetilide-mediated Kv11.1/hERG inhibition, but the retrieved evidence does not connect the highlighted atoms to a binding site, pore interaction, or state-dependent mechanism. Establishing that link requires matched analogs plus direct hERG electrophysiology or binding experiments.

Limitations noted:
- Attribution shows what moved this model score; it does not prove a molecular mechanism, and its stored faithfulness check did not outperform random deletion.
- The external evidence retrieved here supports dofetilide-related Kv11.1 inhibition but does not establish the highlighted structural determinants or a detailed binding mechanism.
- Matched-analog and patch-clamp work is a mechanism-validation experiment, not a safety assessment.
