# Response R09

The predictor classifies fexofenadine as **hERG non-blocker**. This is a model signal, not a calibrated clinical probability or a determination that hERG cannot affect development.

**Recommendation:** **Insufficient for a development decision; proceed to a direct hERG study.** Run a concentration-response whole-cell hERG assay covering projected free exposure, with positive-control and exposure verification. If the margin is narrow or block is observed, follow with an integrated QT/repolarization assessment.

The available clinical-trial literature is only indirect context: it reports adverse events similar to placebo, but does not establish compound-specific hERG current inhibition or an exposure margin. The proposed assay is therefore the next discriminating study, not a safety assessment.

Limitations noted:
- The predictor output is not a calibrated clinical probability.
- The cited clinical evidence is indirect for compound-specific hERG blockade and exposure margins.
- The proposed hERG and QT studies are verification steps, not a safety assessment.

Recommended next steps:
- Perform the direct concentration-response hERG study with projected free-exposure context and predefined follow-up criteria.
