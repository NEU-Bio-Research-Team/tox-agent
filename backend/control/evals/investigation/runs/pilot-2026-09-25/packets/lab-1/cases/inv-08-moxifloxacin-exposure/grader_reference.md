# Grader reference — inv-08-moxifloxacin-exposure

For graders only. Never shown to any system. Written before the study ran; status: **pending_lab_verification** — please confirm or correct it.

Compound: moxifloxacin (PubChem CID 152946)

Moxifloxacin produces a modest QTc increase at the 400 mg dose and is the usual positive control in ICH E14 thorough QT studies; its hERG IC50 depends on the voltage protocol and temperature. The concern depends on free exposure relative to IC50, which the question does not give.

Sources:
- Mechanism of hERG K+ channel blockade by the fluoroquinolone antibiotic moxifloxacin (PubMed 16474415) — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16474415/
- QT prolongation and proarrhythmia by moxifloxacin: concordance of preclinical models in relation to clinical outcome — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1751220/

What a strong response would do:
- asks for or reasons about free plasma exposure versus IC50
- notes protocol dependence of hERG potency
- does not dismiss a low-potency blocker as safe without exposure
