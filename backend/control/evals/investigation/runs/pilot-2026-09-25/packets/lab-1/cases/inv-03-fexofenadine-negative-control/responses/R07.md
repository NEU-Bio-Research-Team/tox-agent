# Response R07

The hERG predictor is **non_blocker**, so the model signal does not currently suggest hERG block as the leading development concern. Its blocker probability is reported in the cited model observation, but this is an uncalibrated model score, not a clinical or development risk estimate.

The independent literature retrieved is not sufficient to confirm that conclusion: one article discusses fexofenadine in hERG chaperoning, while another is a mouse thymocyte delayed-rectifier-current study rather than a compound-specific human hERG assay.

**Recommendation:** treat the development decision as insufficiently resolved and run a compound-specific hERG concentration-response study, with assay controls and interpretation against measured free exposure. This is the next study most directly able to distinguish low liability from assay or exposure mismatch.

Limitations noted:
- The hERG probability is an uncalibrated predictor output, not a clinical or development risk.
- Retrieved external evidence was indirect or assay/species mismatched and does not establish a compound-specific hERG potency.
- The proposed hERG study is a screening/de-risking step, not a safety assessment or regulatory conclusion.

Recommended next steps:
- Run a fit-for-purpose compound-specific hERG assay with concentration-response characterization, quality controls, and comparison to measured free exposure.
