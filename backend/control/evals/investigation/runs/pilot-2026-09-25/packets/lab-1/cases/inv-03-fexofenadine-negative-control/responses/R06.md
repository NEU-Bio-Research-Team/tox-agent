# Response R06

Current evidence does not indicate that hERG liability is likely to block fexofenadine development, but this is not yet a development go/no-go conclusion.

- The predictor classifies fexofenadine as `non_blocker`; its model blocker probability is approximately 25.1%.
- A review specifically covering fexofenadine reported no evidence of cardiotoxicity even at up to four times the standard licensed dose in its reviewed clinical context.
- The strongest next study is a validated functional hERG patch-clamp concentration-response assay, with vehicle and positive controls, concentrations spanning expected free exposure and higher multiples, and matched measured free plasma exposure. This is the study most likely to resolve the remaining assay and exposure uncertainty.

Recommendation: hold a final development decision pending that hERG/exposure package, rather than deprioritizing fexofenadine on the current hERG signal alone.

Limitations noted:
- The blocker probability is a model score, not a calibrated clinical or development risk.
- The applicability check is based on element rules and does not establish broader distributional similarity.
- External evidence is limited to the cited review and contextual experimental study; it does not replace a development-specific functional hERG assessment.
- The proposed assay is a screening and characterization step, not a safety assessment or regulatory conclusion.

Recommended next steps:
- Complete the functional hERG concentration-response and free-exposure study before making a final development decision.
