# Response R04

The concern is **conditional, not decidable from the patch-clamp concentration alone**. The fixed model labels moxifloxacin `non_blocker` and gives a blocker score of **0.143**; this is an uncalibrated model signal, not a clinical exposure or QT-risk estimate.

What would settle therapeutic relevance:
- measured human **unbound** plasma exposure, including peak and accumulation scenarios;
- hERG concentration-response data from a standardized assay reported on a free-concentration basis;
- the resulting exposure margin, including relevant interaction and organ-impairment scenarios;
- exposure-matched ECG/QTc data, ideally therapeutic and supratherapeutic.

Until those are linked, keep the hERG finding as a screening concern and do not use it to claim or dismiss clinical risk. The bounded external search in this run did not establish a directly applicable compound-specific exposure relationship.

Limitations noted:
- The hERG score is a model score, not a calibrated clinical probability.
- The proposed exposure comparison and ECG/QTc work are verification steps, not a safety assessment.
- The bounded external search did not establish a directly applicable compound-specific exposure relationship; a missing retrieved record is not evidence of absence.

Recommended next steps:
- Obtain free human Cmax and exposure distributions, reproduce hERG concentration-response under matched assay conditions, and compare with exposure-matched QTc/ECG measurements.
