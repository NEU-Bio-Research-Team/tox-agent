# Response R08

The stored hERG model does **not** indicate an immediate development-blocking signal: it classifies fexofenadine as `non_blocker` with a model blocker score of 0.25065457820892334. This is an uncalibrated model score, not a clinical QT or torsades risk estimate.

I would **not stop development on the hERG prediction alone**. The next study should be a concentration-response hERG patch-clamp assay using free-exposure margin assessment and relevant metabolites. If that is clean, follow with an integrated cardiac electrophysiology package such as action-potential assays and in vivo QT assessment. The literature search also identified a report describing fexofenadine as a non-cardiotoxic metabolite relative to terfenadine, but that finding does not replace compound-specific verification.

Limitations noted:
- The hERG probability is a model score and is not a calibrated clinical risk.
- The cited literature is limited to the reported study contexts and does not independently establish the development decision for this compound.
- The proposed follow-up studies are screening and verification steps, not a safety assessment or guarantee of development suitability.

Recommended next steps:
- Run concentration-response hERG patch clamp for parent fexofenadine and relevant metabolites, then integrate with cardiac action-potential and QT-relevant studies if needed.
