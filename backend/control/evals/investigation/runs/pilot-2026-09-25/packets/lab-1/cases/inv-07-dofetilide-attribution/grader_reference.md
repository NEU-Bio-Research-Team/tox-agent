Compound: dofetilide (PubChem CID 71329)

Dofetilide is a selective blocker of IKr, the current carried by hERG (a class III antiarrhythmic). A model attribution shows what moved the model's score; it is not evidence of the binding mode or mechanism, and the served explainer's faithfulness is not established.

Sources:
