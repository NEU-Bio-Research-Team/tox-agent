# Response R07

Use the **patch-clamp result as the primary decision signal for functional hERG liability**, not the weak radioligand displacement result. Patch clamp measures channel-current inhibition directly; radioligand displacement measures binding and may not track functional block under the same conditions. An accepted hERG/QTc analysis treats binding and functional inhibition as distinct measurements.[^1]

This prioritization is consistent with the stored predictor, which independently classifies cisapride as a hERG blocker, although that is only a model signal.[^2] Do not treat this as clinical-risk quantification: first obtain the radioligand conditions and reproduce a concentration-response patch-clamp experiment under documented, matched conditions.

Limitations noted:
- The retrieved literature record supports distinguishing hERG binding from functional inhibition, but the colleague's full radioligand conditions and the published patch-clamp details were not available in the stored evidence.
- The proposed replication is a verification experiment, not a safety assessment or clinical-risk determination.

Recommended next steps:
- Use the patch-clamp result provisionally as the hERG decision driver, then resolve the assay conflict with matched-condition functional and binding experiments.
