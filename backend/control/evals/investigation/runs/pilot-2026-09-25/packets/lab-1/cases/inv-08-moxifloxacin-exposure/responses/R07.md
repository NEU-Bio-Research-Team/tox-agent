# Response R07

The result is a concern warranting exposure-matched evaluation, but it does not establish a therapeutic-exposure hERG risk.

- The fixed predictor labels moxifloxacin `non_blocker`; its blocker score is 0.143. This is an uncalibrated model score, not a clinical probability.
- The reported patch-clamp concentration cannot be compared directly with therapeutic exposure unless the comparison uses unbound human plasma concentrations and matching assay conditions. Published translational work on moxifloxacin found substantial functional-assay variability and unreliable in-vitro-to-in-vivo translation.
- The decision is currently **insufficient**, not reassuring or adverse.

What would settle it:
1. Human PK for the intended regimen: unbound peak and trough concentrations, including renal impairment and interacting medicines.
2. A protocol-defined functional hERG concentration-response curve for moxifloxacin, with temperature, recording configuration, incubation, and free-versus-total concentration handling specified.
3. Exposure-QTc data, ideally including concentration-QTc modelling and relevant high-exposure populations.

These tests are screening and translational assessments, not a standalone safety determination.

Limitations noted:
- The predictor score is an uncalibrated model output, not a clinical probability.
- External evidence was limited to the retrieved literature records and does not supply the matched human free-exposure comparison needed here.
- The proposed PK, hERG, and QTc work is screening and translational assessment, not a standalone safety determination.

Recommended next steps:
- Obtain unbound human peak/trough PK for the intended regimen, repeat or document a protocol-defined functional hERG concentration-response curve, and integrate exposure with concentration-QTc data.
