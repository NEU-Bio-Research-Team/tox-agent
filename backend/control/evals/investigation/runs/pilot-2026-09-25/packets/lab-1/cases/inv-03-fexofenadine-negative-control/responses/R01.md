# Response R01

**hERG is unlikely to block development of fexofenadine at conventional exposure.** Published hERG IC₅₀ values are about **23–65 µM**, while a 180 mg dose produces a peak plasma concentration of roughly **1 µM total** (about **0.3–0.4 µM unbound**). Clinical studies at substantially higher doses found no dose related QTc increase. This is a meaningful separation, although an FDA review noted that the older ECG data could not exclude a small QTc effect. [FDA cardiac safety review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/201373Orig1s000OtherR.pdf), [hERG study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12070109/), [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2005/020872s015lbl.pdf), [clinical study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10335761/).

**Next study:** run a concentration–response **whole cell hERG patch clamp assay** on the intended material at physiological temperature. Verify the concentrations reaching the cells and include a positive control. Compare the result with the *highest plausible unbound exposure*, including exposure increases from renal impairment or drug interactions. That gives the team a program specific margin for the decision; current evidence supports **proceeding**, subject to that check. [ICH E14/S7B guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/e14-and-s7b-clinical-and-nonclinical-evaluation-qtqtc-interval-prolongation-and-proarrhythmic).
