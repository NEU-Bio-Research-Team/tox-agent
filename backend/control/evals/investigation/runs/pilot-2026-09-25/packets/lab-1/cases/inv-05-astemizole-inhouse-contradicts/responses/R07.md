## Reply to turn 1

Astemizole should be treated as a **high-priority hERG concern** in this repurposing screen. The fixed predictor labels it **blocker** and assigns a high blocker probability. This is a model signal, not a calibrated clinical risk or proof of mechanism.

The result supports holding further development decisions pending a compound-specific concentration-response hERG assay and comparison with the intended use's free-exposure margin. That test is a screening step, not a safety assessment. The bounded literature search did not establish independent, compound-specific hERG evidence for astemizole, so the concern remains conditional rather than experimentally confirmed.

Limitations noted:
- The hERG probability is a predictor score and is not a calibrated clinical risk estimate.
- A concentration-response hERG assay and exposure-margin comparison are screening and verification steps, not a safety assessment.
- The bounded literature search did not establish independent, compound-specific hERG evidence for astemizole; this absence is not evidence of no liability.

Recommended next steps:
- Run a concentration-response hERG assay for astemizole and compare the measured potency with the intended repurposing use's free systemic exposure.
