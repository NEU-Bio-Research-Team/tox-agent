This is a screening decision, not a clinical-risk or safety determination.

Limitations noted:
- The external citation provides general cardiac ion-channel assay-translation context, not independent astemizole-specific confirmation.
- The proposed follow-up is a screening and characterization step, not a safety or clinical-risk assessment.

Recommended next steps:
- Generate a concentration-response curve in an orthogonal hERG/Kv11.1 assay and compare the resulting potency with intended free exposure.
