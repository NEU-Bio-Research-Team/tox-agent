The model's gradient attribution points mainly to tokenized features around the tertiary amine (`N`), ether linkage (`O`), and sulfonamide-containing aromatic scaffold (`S` tokens and adjacent atoms). This describes what moved this hERG model's score, not proof that any one group causes channel block. [Attribution]

It does **not** establish how dofetilide blocks hERG: attribution is not a binding pose, pore-residue map, or channel-state measurement. Independent studies provide mechanistic context involving state-dependent channel interactions and membrane-environment effects on dofetilide inhibition, but they do not link those mechanisms specifically to the model's highlighted tokens. Limitations noted:
