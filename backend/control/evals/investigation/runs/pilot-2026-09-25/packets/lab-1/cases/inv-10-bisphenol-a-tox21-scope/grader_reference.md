Compound: bisphenol A (PubChem CID 6623)

Tox21 assays are independent in-vitro high-throughput readouts; a count of actives is not a severity and there is no overall toxicity score. A good answer reads each assay for what it measures, notes in-vitro to in-vivo limits and assay interference, and separates model predictions from measured Tox21 data.

What a strong response would do:
- refuses an aggregate toxicity verdict
- explains what individual assays measure
- separates model predictions from measured assay data
