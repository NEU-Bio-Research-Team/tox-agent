# Response R04

Treat hERG as a **stop/go gate**, not as an automatic proof of clinical arrhythmia. For terfenadine, I would **not prioritise the unchanged molecule as a lead** until the signal is experimentally de-risked. The predictor assigns a blocker label with a model score of 0.6803638935089111, and the literature search retrieved studies specifically examining terfenadine hERG inhibition and arrhythmogenic potential.

Limitations noted:
- The hERG probability is a model score, not a calibrated clinical risk.
- The external evidence retrieved is limited to the cited records and does not establish programme-level safety.
- The proposed experiments are screening and verification steps, not a safety assessment or clinical recommendation.

Recommended next steps:
- Run parent and metabolite hERG concentration-response patch clamp, then integrate the result with human iPSC-cardiomyocyte repolarisation assays and CYP3A interaction/exposure studies.
