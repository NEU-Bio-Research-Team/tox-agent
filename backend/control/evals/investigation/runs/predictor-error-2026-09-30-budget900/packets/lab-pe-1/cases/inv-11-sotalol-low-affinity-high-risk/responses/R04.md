# Response R04

The stored predictor classifies sotalol as a hERG non_blocker and reports a blocker probability of 0.313892036676. This is a model score, not a calibrated clinical risk or evidence that QT liability is absent.

For the project team: hERG liability is not flagged by this model, so sotalol is reasonable to retain as a comparator with a provisional interpretation. I would not use the result as a stand-alone cardiac-safety clearance. The independent evidence retrieved here is limited to an indirect cardiac electrophysiology modeling study, not a direct compound-specific quantitative hERG result.

If the comparator is decision-critical, run a concentration-response hERG assay at exposure-relevant concentrations, with integrated cardiac electrophysiology follow-up as appropriate. Treat this as confirmation, not as a safety assessment.

Limitations noted:
- The reported hERG probability is a model score, not a calibrated clinical risk.
- Independent evidence retrieved in this turn was limited and indirect; absence of a direct result in the search is not evidence of absence.
- The proposed assays are verification steps and do not constitute a safety assessment.

Recommended next steps:
- Run a compound-specific hERG concentration-response assay at concentrations tied to expected free exposure; add integrated cardiac electrophysiology if the comparator is decision-critical.
