# Response R04

Catechin’s Tox21 profile is **mixed and worth follow-up, not a toxicity verdict**.

- The model flags activity in **NR-AhR**, **NR-Aromatase**, **NR-ER/ER-LBD**, **SR-ARE**, **SR-HSE**, **SR-MMP**, and **SR-p53**; it does not flag NR-AR, NR-AR-LBD, NR-PPAR-gamma, or SR-ATAD5. These are independent assay predictions, not a severity count.
- The strongest practical interpretation is a **screening signal for possible receptor-, stress-response-, and mitochondrial-pathway activity**. It does not establish mechanism, in-vivo hazard, clinical risk, or safety.
- The reported scores are model outputs and are not calibrated probabilities. The available external catechin paper is indirect and does not independently validate these Tox21 predictions. [cite]

**Action:** use an **insufficient-to-decide** posture for development based on this profile alone. Prioritize concentration-response confirmation for the flagged assays, using orthogonal readouts, cytotoxicity controls, assay-interference controls, and exposure context. Treat the result as a prioritization screen, not a safety assessment.

Limitations noted:
- Any model probabilities or activity scores are uncalibrated model outputs, not clinical or real-world risks.
- External evidence was limited; the retrieved article was indirect and no direct independent Tox21 validation was established.
- The proposed confirmation work is screening and verification, not a safety assessment.

Recommended next steps:
- Run concentration-response confirmation for the flagged assays with orthogonal readouts, cytotoxicity controls, assay-interference controls, and exposure context.
